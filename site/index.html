A FEW USEFUL ANSWERS

Before we
build together.

A clear conversation is the best starting point.

Can we start with a small project?

Yes. We can begin by discussing one business problem, agreeing a focused scope and exploring a prototype before a larger build. The team will confirm what can be delivered and the associated cost.

Are the SaaS products available now?

Only products marked Available in the catalogue are presented as ready to explore. Products marked Coming soon are still in development. Ask the team for confirmed features, pricing and launch details.

Can you work with our existing systems?

Integration needs are part of the discovery conversation. Tell us which tools you use and what needs to connect, so we can assess the requirements before making a commitment.

Do you provide AI and IoT solutions?

AI and IoT are part of our long-term direction. Specific capabilities, availability and delivery commitments should be confirmed with the team rather than assumed from that vision.

How do I get a project estimate?

Send an enquiry or contact us on WhatsApp. Include your business need, key features, existing systems and preferred timing. We will use those details to discuss an appropriate next step.

We use your contact details to respond to your enquiry. Please don’t include passwords or sensitive documents.

Your privacy.

When you send an enquiry, RIPEES stores your name, contact details, business name and message so the team can respond. The owner can manage these records and private follow-up notes in the protected workspace.

On the local test service, records are saved on this computer. On the hosted service, records are saved in the website’s database. Published website and product information is public; enquiry records and internal notes are excluded from public pages and chatbot knowledge.

Chat history remains in the current page until refresh or reset and is not automatically saved to the CRM. Usage metadata and feedback follow a 30-day retention policy, with expired records cleaned during use. Questions are shared with the owner only when you explicitly choose to share them; enquiry summaries are reviewed before submission. When AI mode is enabled, messages are sent to the connected AI provider. Do not submit passwords or sensitive documents.

Owner sign-in uses an authentication cookie. The local owner button is for testing only; the hosted service uses ChatGPT sign-in. Hosting infrastructure may process technical request information. This site does not use advertising analytics.

To ask about access, correction or deletion of your enquiry, contact RIPEES through the enquiry form or the phone and WhatsApp details on this website.
