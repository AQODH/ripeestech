A FEW USEFUL ANSWERS

Before we
build together.

A clear conversation is the best starting point.

Ask us on WhatsApp ↗
Can we start with a small project?

Yes. We can begin by discussing one business problem, agreeing a focused scope and exploring a prototype before a larger build. The team will confirm what can be delivered and the associated cost.

Are the SaaS products available now?

Only products marked Available in the catalogue are presented as ready to explore. Products marked Coming soon are still in development. Ask the team for confirmed features, pricing and launch details.

Can you work with our existing systems?

Integration needs are part of the discovery conversation. Tell us which tools you use and what needs to connect, so we can assess the requirements before making a commitment.

Do you provide AI and IoT solutions?

AI and IoT are part of our long-term direction. Specific capabilities, availability and delivery commitments should be confirmed with the team rather than assumed from that vision.

How do I get a project estimate?

Send an enquiry or contact us on WhatsApp. Include your business need, key features, existing systems and preferred timing. We will use those details to discuss an appropriate next step.

LET’S START WITH YOUR IDEA

Your next chapter.

Tell us what you need. Your enquiry will be saved securely for the RIPEES team to follow up.

We use your contact details to respond to your enquiry. Please don’t include passwords or sensitive documents.

Your privacy.

When you send an enquiry, your name, contact details, business name and message are stored in the RIPEES website’s shared database so the company can respond. The owner can view and manage these records in the protected administration area.

Only published product information and website content are shown to visitors. Internal enquiry notes and contact records are not public. The site uses ChatGPT sign-in to protect owner tools and hosting infrastructure processes standard technical information to deliver the website.

The website assistant uses published company information and approved answers. If AI mode is connected, visitor messages are sent to the AI provider to generate a reply. Chat transcripts are not automatically saved in the CRM. Temporary hashed request counters help limit abuse. This site does not use advertising analytics. Contact RIPEES using the website’s enquiry form with questions about your information.
